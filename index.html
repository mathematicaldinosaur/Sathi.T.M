About

I am an Integrated PhD student in mathematics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Thiruvananthapuram, advised by Dr. Shrihari Sridharan. My research is in complex dynamics and ergodic theory, with a focus on holomorphic correspondences, the multi-valued analogues of holomorphic maps. I am also interested in thermodynamic formalism and complex geometry.
